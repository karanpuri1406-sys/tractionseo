SEO & AI Glossary

Your complete A-Z guide to SEO and AI terminology

AI (Artificial Intelligence)

Technology that enables machines to simulate human intelligence. In SEO, AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude help with content creation, keyword research, and optimization.

Algorithm

A set of rules used by search engines like Google to rank web pages. Google's algorithm considers 200+ factors including content quality, backlinks, and user experience.

Alt Text

Alternative text description for images that helps search engines understand image content and improves accessibility. Essential for image SEO.

Anchor Text

The clickable text in a hyperlink. Using relevant anchor text helps search engines understand what the linked page is about.

Backlink

A link from another website to your website. High-quality backlinks are one of the most important ranking factors in Google's algorithm.

Black Hat SEO

Unethical SEO practices that violate search engine guidelines, such as keyword stuffing or buying links. Can result in penalties.

Bounce Rate

Percentage of visitors who leave your website after viewing only one page. High bounce rates may indicate poor user experience or irrelevant content.

ChatGPT

AI language model by OpenAI used for content creation, keyword research, and SEO strategy. Popular tool for automating SEO tasks.

Citation

Mention of your business name, address, and phone number (NAP) on other websites. Important for local SEO in India.

Claude

AI assistant by Anthropic, excellent for long-form content creation and research. Alternative to ChatGPT with strong analytical capabilities.

Content Cluster

Group of related content pieces linked together around a central topic. Helps establish topical authority and improves SEO.

Core Web Vitals

Google's metrics for measuring user experience: Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), First Input Delay (FID), and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).

Crawling

Process where search engine bots discover and scan web pages. Proper site structure helps bots crawl your site efficiently.

Domain Authority (DA)

Score (0-100) predicting how well a website will rank. Higher DA indicates stronger ranking potential. Developed by Moz.

Duplicate Content

Identical or very similar content appearing on multiple pages or websites. Can hurt SEO rankings if not handled properly.

E-E-A-T

Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness. Google's quality guidelines for evaluating content, especially important for YMYL topics.

Entity SEO

Optimization based on entities (people, places, things) rather than just keywords. Important for semantic search and AI-powered search.

Featured Snippet

Special search result box appearing at the top of Google (position zero). Provides direct answers to user queries.

Frase

AI-powered SEO content optimization tool. Helps create content briefs and optimize existing content for better rankings.

Google Business Profile (GBP)

Free listing on Google for local businesses. Essential for local SEO in India. Formerly known as Google My Business.

Google Search Console

Free tool from Google showing how your site performs in search. Essential for monitoring rankings, clicks, and technical issues.

H1 Tag

Main heading tag on a webpage. Should contain your primary keyword and clearly describe the page content.

Hreflang Tag

HTML attribute indicating language and regional targeting. Important for multilingual sites targeting different Indian languages.

Indexing

Process where search engines store and organize web pages in their database. Pages must be indexed to appear in search results.

Internal Linking

Links between pages on your own website. Helps distribute page authority and improves site navigation.

Jasper AI

AI writing assistant for marketing content. Popular for creating SEO-optimized blog posts, ads, and social media content.

Keyword

Words or phrases users type into search engines. Foundation of SEO strategy—target keywords your audience actually searches for.

Keyword Density

Percentage of times a keyword appears in content. Aim for natural usage (1-2%) rather than keyword stuffing.

Keyword Difficulty

Metric indicating how hard it is to rank for a keyword. Consider difficulty vs. search volume when choosing keywords.

Link Building

Process of acquiring backlinks from other websites. One of the most important ranking factors in SEO.

Local SEO

Optimization for local search results. Critical for businesses serving specific geographic areas in India.

Long-Tail Keyword

Longer, more specific keyword phrases (3+ words). Lower competition and higher conversion rates than short keywords.

LSI Keywords

Latent Semantic Indexing keywords—terms related to your main keyword. Help search engines understand content context.

Meta Description

Brief description (155-160 characters) appearing in search results. Doesn't directly affect rankings but influences click-through rates.

Mobile-First Indexing

Google primarily uses mobile version of content for indexing and ranking. Essential for Indian market where 85%+ searches are mobile.

NAP

Name, Address, Phone number. Must be consistent across all online platforms for local SEO success.

Natural Language Processing (NLP)

AI technology helping computers understand human language. Used by Google to better understand search queries and content.

Nofollow Link

Link with rel="nofollow" attribute telling search engines not to pass authority. Used for paid links or untrusted content.

On-Page SEO

Optimization of elements on your website including content, HTML tags, images, and internal links.

Organic Traffic

Visitors coming from unpaid search results. The goal of SEO is to increase organic traffic.

Page Speed

How quickly your page loads. Critical ranking factor, especially for mobile. Aim for under 3 seconds load time.

Prompt Engineering

Art of crafting effective instructions for AI tools like ChatGPT. Better prompts = better AI-generated content.

Ranking

Position of your webpage in search results. Higher rankings = more visibility and traffic.

Robots.txt

File telling search engine crawlers which pages to crawl or ignore on your website.

Schema Markup

Structured data code helping search engines understand your content. Can result in rich snippets in search results.

Search Intent

The reason behind a user's search query. Four types: informational, navigational, commercial, transactional.

Semantic Search

Search technology understanding meaning and context, not just keywords. Powered by AI and NLP.

SERP (Search Engine Results Page)

Page showing search results after a query. Includes organic results, ads, featured snippets, and other features.

Sitemap

XML file listing all pages on your website. Helps search engines discover and crawl your content.

Surfer SEO

AI-powered content optimization tool. Analyzes top-ranking pages and provides recommendations for your content.

Technical SEO

Optimization of website infrastructure including site speed, mobile-friendliness, crawlability, and indexing.

Title Tag

HTML element specifying page title. Appears in search results and browser tabs. Should include primary keyword.

URL Structure

Format of your webpage addresses. Clean, descriptive URLs help both users and search engines understand content.

User Experience (UX)

Overall experience visitors have on your website. Good UX improves rankings and conversions.

Voice Search

Searching using voice commands. Growing rapidly in India with Google Assistant. Optimize for natural language queries.

White Hat SEO

Ethical SEO practices following search engine guidelines. Sustainable approach for long-term success.

XML Sitemap

File listing all important pages on your site for search engines. Submit to Google Search Console for better crawling.

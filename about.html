About TractionSEO

Empowering Indian businesses with AI-powered SEO

Our Mission

TractionSEO was created with a simple mission: to democratize AI-powered SEO for Indian businesses. We believe that every entrepreneur, small business owner, and professional in India deserves access to world-class SEO knowledge and tools—without breaking the bank.

Why We Focus on India

India's digital landscape is unique. With over 700 million internet users, 85%+ mobile searches, and 22 official languages, Indian businesses face challenges that generic SEO advice doesn't address. We understand:

  • The importance of mobile-first optimization in a mobile-dominated market
  • The growing significance of regional language SEO
  • The unique local search behavior in Indian cities
  • The budget constraints of Indian startups and SMBs
  • The need for practical, actionable strategies over theory

📅 Month 4 - Scaling Up (Income: ₹28,000)

  • Published 40 more articles

What Arjun Did Right (You Can Copy This)

  1. Chose a niche he understood: As a student, he knew what students searched for
  2. Started with low competition keywords: "Best free apps for..." instead of "Best apps"
  3. Used AI to scale content: Created 20-30 articles per month using ChatGPT
  4. Showed up consistently: Attended every Lab coaching call, asked questions
  5. Monetized early: Added AdSense in month 2, didn't wait for "perfect" traffic
  6. Reinvested profits: Upgraded hosting, bought SEO tools, hired a VA
